How these figures are calculated

Transfer duty (standard)Revenue NSW 2025/26 marginal rates; premium duty above $3,721,000
FHB stamp duty concessionFHBAS: $0 up to $800k; sliding scale $800,001–$999,999 (official table from 1 Jul 2025)
5% Deposit SchemeMin 5% of property value; NSW caps $1.5M (metro centres) / $800k (other) from 1 Oct 2025
LVRLoan ÷ purchase price — based on deposit only; stamp duty and fees are separate (lender may use valuation if different)
Monthly repaymentsStandard P&I amortisation; max 30-year term per scheme guidance
LMI estimateIndicative % of loan by LVR band if above 80%; $0 under First Home Guarantee
Cash bufferAdjustable; default is greater of $20,000 or 3× monthly repayment
Conveyancing estimateNSW typical professional fees ~$1,300–$2,400 by price (industry guides 2025/26)
Search estimateDisbursements ~$300–$650 by price (title, council, water, rates certificates)
Preparedness extrasMoving, pest inspection, building & home insurance (1st year), council rates — indicative defaults by price
Not includedLMI (waived if scheme applies), foreign buyer 9% surcharge, FHOG grant, PEXA (~$130+), strata levies, ongoing utilities

Sources: Revenue NSW transfer duty, NSW FHBAS, 5% Deposit Scheme, NSW Land Registry Services.
